# Products-Wise Sales Analysis Specification

## 1. Executive Overview

The **Products Wise Sales Analysis** module provides multi-dimensional product and category performance insights for the Sales Intelligence Platform (SIP). It enables business leaders and sales directors to evaluate sales results across both **Monetary Value (Sales Amount)** and **Volume / Quantity (% Share)**, with dedicated category comparison intelligence (e.g., **Detergent vs. Others**).

---

## 2. Key Analytical Capabilities

### 2.1 Dual-Metric Mode (Value vs. Percentage Share)
Every product visualization and tabular view supports seamless switching between:
- **Value-Based View**: Raw monetary sales amount (MTD Sales Revenue in currency units).
- **Percentage (% Share) View**: Relative contribution of each product SKU or product group relative to total sales.

### 2.2 Category Comparison Engine (Detergent vs. Others)
A specialized category breakdown engine isolates key product lines for comparative performance evaluation:
- **Target Category Sales Value**: Total sales amount generated by the target category (e.g., Detergent).
- **Target Category Value Share %**: `(Target Category Sales / Total Company Sales) × 100`
- **Others Sales Value**: Total sales amount generated by all non-target categories.
- **Others Value Share %**: `(Others Sales / Total Company Sales) × 100`
- **Target Category Volume & Volume Share %**: Corresponding physical quantity metrics and volume percentages.

---

## 3. Metric & KPI Formulas

| Metric Name | Formula / Logic | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Category Sales Value** | `∑ (Sales Amount for SKU ∈ Category)` | Total currency revenue generated by category |
| **Category Sales Value Share %** | `(Category Sales Value / Total Sales Value) × 100` | Percentage of total company revenue contributed by category |
| **Category Volume** | `∑ (Quantity for SKU ∈ Category)` | Total physical units sold in source unit policy |
| **Category Volume Share %** | `(Category Volume / Total Company Volume) × 100` | Percentage share of physical unit volume |
| **Detergent vs. Others Ratio** | `Detergent Sales Value / Others Sales Value` | Relative monetary size of Detergent vs. remaining catalog |
| **Pareto Cumulative Share %** | `Running Sum(SKU Value) / Total Sales Value × 100` | Identifies top 80/20 revenue driver SKUs |

---

## 4. UI & Visual Components

### 4.1 Category Comparison Doughnut & Bar Gauge
- Displays visual share of **Detergent** versus **Others** with percentage callouts and value tooltips.
- Interactive mode toggle allows switching between Monetary Value (Currency) and Percentage (% Share).

### 4.2 Product Group Distribution Chart
- Multi-bar visual ranking all product categories (Detergent, Soap, Personal Care, Household, etc.).
- Sortable by Sales Value or Volume Share.

### 4.3 Detailed Product Analysis Register
- Filterable data table displaying:
  1. SKU Name & Pack Size
  2. Category / Group
  3. MTD Sales Value & Value Share %
  4. Unit Quantity & Volume Share %
  5. Category Rank & Pareto Classification (A/B/C)

---

## 5. Technical Integration Architecture

- **Backend Aggregator**: `SIP.KpiEngine` (Google Apps Script) computes category and product contracts during certification.
- **API Payload**: `SIP.WebApi` delivers `productAnalysis` objects containing pre-calculated category aggregates and comparative ratios.
- **Frontend Renderer**: `BI.Charts` and `BI.Tables` render canvas visuals and interactive tables in `src/html/`.
